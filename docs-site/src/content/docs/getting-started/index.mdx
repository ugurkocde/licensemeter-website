Use this guide to set up your own workspace. If you only want to explore, take the separate [sample-workspace tour](/getting-started/sample-workspace/). No account or provider consent is needed for that tour.

## Choose your starting point

| Your situation | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| You are setting up LicenseMeter for an organization | [Before you start](/getting-started/before-you-start/), then [Sign in and find your workspace](/getting-started/sign-in/) |

## Your first complete workflow

<Steps>

1. ## Prepare access

   Read [Before you start](/getting-started/before-you-start/). Decide who will own the workspace, which Microsoft tenant you will assess, and whether you will connect continuously or start with a snapshot.

2. ## Sign in and confirm the workspace

   [Sign in](/getting-started/sign-in/) using the identity intended for this workspace. Check the workspace name and your role. A new empty workspace shows **Add your Microsoft 365 directory**. If your organization already uses LicenseMeter, request an invitation instead of creating a duplicate connection.

3. ## Add Microsoft data

   Choose one path: [managed consent](/connectors/microsoft-managed/), [your own app registration](/connectors/microsoft-byo/), [CSV import](/getting-started/csv-import/), or an [instant scan](/getting-started/instant-scan/) where available. These are alternatives, not four steps you must complete.

   Other provider connectors require a live Microsoft connection. A CSV import or instant scan provides a Microsoft assessment but does not unlock those connectors.

4. ## Verify the result

   Follow [Verify your first sync](/getting-started/first-sync/). Check the workspace, source freshness, Sync history and Detection capabilities. Reaching Overview does not prove the sync was complete: partial runs can also take you there.

5. ## Set prices and review one finding

   Use [Review your first finding](/getting-started/first-review/) to enter a contract price, inspect the evidence, record a decision and export a report. You can complete this review without changing anything at a provider.

6. ## Bring in your team and other sources

   [Invite colleagues](/workspace/members/) with the access they need. For continuous monitoring, add further [connectors](/connectors/) after the live Microsoft connection is working. Review [Settings](/workspace/settings/) for notifications and sync history.

</Steps>

## You are ready when

- You can identify the active workspace and your role.
- You know whether its data is live, imported or from a one-time scan.
- You checked source coverage and the latest sync or import.
- You reviewed the price basis and at least one finding's evidence.
- You know who owns the next action and how to share a report.

Zero findings alone is not a completion criterion. Missing data, permissions or prices can make an incomplete assessment look quiet.
