# Explore the sample workspace

Open [LicenseMeter](https://www.licensemeter.com/) and select **Open the sample tenant**. No sign-in account, provider keys or Microsoft admin consent are needed.

1. Confirm **Demo workspace** in the sidebar or mobile menu.
2. In **Overview**, inspect Monthly waste, Price accuracy and Next best actions. These are fictional figures.
3. Open **Findings**, choose a finding and inspect its evidence and user context. Workflow controls may display a preview.
4. Open **Licenses & prices** to see the price basis. Demo changes are not saved.
5. Browse **Connectors** to learn what each provider supplies. Sample connection status does not represent access to any real provider.
6. Select **PDF report** to inspect a demonstration export. Keep its sample-data context when sharing it.

## Move to your own organization

Select **Sign out**, then follow [Sign in and find your workspace](sign-in.md). The sample data is not converted into your organization's workspace. A real new workspace starts empty, and its results depend on the sources you connect or import.

Use **Settings > Product tour** to replay the in-app walkthrough where available. The documentation's [first-review guide](first-review.md) supplies the full sequence beyond the tour.
